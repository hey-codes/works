# DESIGN.md

Visual system for codymitch.works (Atlas v26). Source of truth for values: `src/app/globals.css`. This copy is corrected against the v26 design reference; where the two ever disagree, the code wins.

## Color

| Token | Light | Dark | Job |

| bg | #ffffff | #171614 | page |
| soft | #fbfaf9 | #1b1a18 | hover ground, map sea |

| blue | #0070e0 | #4da3ff | **interaction and data only** |
| red | #e10b00 | #ff453a | **structure, an open role's rail, the pen line under the hero name and an open role's name, the AWARD chips, and the map's project marks** |

The rule: if you can click it, it is blue; if it marks where something begins or belongs, it is red. Never crossed, with two exceptions. (Cody, 2026-10-14) Red also marks Cody's projects on the map (a new site opening or a site in construction): one red diamond, on a site or as the notch on the rim of a circle that holds one, the site card's PROJECT flag and the legend's diamonds. The marks on sites and circles are clickable and still red; nothing else on the map is red. (Cody, 2026-10-15) Red also marks an open role's rail: an open record is structure, its start marked as the section dash is. Red also marks the AWARD chips in DETAILS, outline and text, as a project flag is; their key stays muted (Cody, 2026-10-15). Red also draws the pen line under the hero name, where the page begins, and under an open role's name, joining its red rail; the one blue pen line is the school name's, on hover and focus (Cody, 2026-10-15). Focus rings are always blue.

The dark palette is warm charcoal, picked for the dark ground rather than inverted from light. The theme follows the system setting until the user picks one.

The map is the one place Tailwind and shadcn naming appear (`src/app/tailwind.css` maps shadcn's color names onto these tokens). CARTO's country, state and continent names wait for zoom 9, where the market circles give way to sites, so none reads as a circle's name (`regionLabelPlan`). The basemap is CARTO's, recolored from these tokens on every style load, both themes (`groundPaint` in `src/lib/basemap.ts`): land is `land`, the sea `soft` with a 1px coastline in `edge`, parks and land cover flat in the land, built-up areas a trace of ink, borders grey (`edge`, `under`, `line`; CARTO's pink dashes would read as a red mark), roads outlined in `under` and `line` and filled with the land, and CARTO's names `muted` mixed 30% toward the land, a step quieter than the map's own market names. No red or blue in the ground. The Honolulu inset's ground takes the same recolor, with every CARTO name hidden. On the blank ground (CARTO unreachable) there is nothing to recolor.

## Structure patterns

- **Section head:** a red 20×4 dash (drawn in the first time it scrolls into view; see Motion), an ink mono eyebrow `0N` (optionally `0N · CONTEXT`), the Fraunces title, then a 1px ink rule. Eyebrow is not blue: it is not clickable. Fixed order: 01 Highlights, 02 Experience, 03 Education, 04 Capabilities, 05 Stack.
- **Row grid:** every section uses `clamp(72px,20%,120px) minmax(0,1fr)`: mono key or years on the left, content on the right, 16px vertical padding, line hairline between rows. Under 480px the key sits over its content with 4px between them and the content takes the full width, as the role rows' years do, with 12px vertical padding (STACK's CMMS and APPS keys likewise sit over their rows). An open record's SPEC keeps its two columns there, SECTOR | CMMS over FOOTPRINT | BASE, 16px apart, each key over its value and a long value wrapping inside its column (Cody, 2026-10-15).
- **Spacing** (Cody, 2026-10-15, tightened for go-live): 44px above each section head, 32px on phones; 32px between the last section and the footer, 24px on phones. Hero: 10px from the name to the tagline, 6px to the base line, 24px to the stats' hairline, 12px from the hairline to the figures. An open record's bands start 18px under their rules, SPEC keeps 18px under its grid, and the record closes with 12px. Phones: an 8px header, the hero 12px under it and the map 32px under the hero, 6px between a section's dash line and its title and 10px under the title, role rows at 12px with 2px between their lines, band bodies 9px under their rules, STACK rows at 6px. Collapsed role rows keep 16px from 480px up. From 960px the sticky map column keeps the same 32px above the footer, so the caption never sits on its hairline. Under 380px on touch the header's boxes draw at 40px, 4px apart, each keeping a 44px hit area (Cody, 2026-10-15).
- **Stat strip:** a 1px top hairline in `line` spanning the column (the pen line under the name is the hero's one red mark; Cody, 2026-10-15), four fixed figures (13, 400+, 2.8M+, 9 Brands) in a 4-column grid sized by container query, mono labels underneath. On phones the four stay on one line in columns sized to their labels, spread to the edges, so no label wraps; where the strip is under 340px wide (a 360 phone) the labels would sit closer than 16px, near a word space, and the figures take two rows of two. Counts up once over 900ms with an ease-out cubic; skipped under reduced motion and snapped before print.
- **Role record:** Fraunces company with a blue ↗ after it, mono title, mono caps tagline (sector · CMMS · sq ft), site count right-aligned, blue 16px plus icon that rotates to a close mark, then a one-line mono context note (`› first FM hire for the Americas…`, 12px muted, lowercase). The ↗ is the brand's site (`link`; Independent has none and shows none; a name that holds two brands has `links` instead and carries a ↗ after each brand's own word, "J.Crew ↗ and Madewell ↗", each opening that brand's site): an 11px drawn arrow on the name's baseline, a little under its 13px cap height, a link of its own that opens a new tab and leaves the row as it was, named for the company alone, its underline drawing in on hover, its focus ring blue; it does not print. Expanded: Geist highlight with bold figures at 650, muted Geist brief, and DETAILS, SPEC's sibling: a row per key (`SKILL`, `TOOLS`, `AWARD`, in that order, a key with nothing in it showing no row) with the key in SPEC's muted 11px label and its values as chips, mono 13px 500 in ink, outlined in the 1px hairline with no fill, 2px by 6px, 6px apart, that wrap whole and break inside only if one alone is wider than the band (a guard for 320; no chip today is). The role's projects are not listed in the record; picking the role shows them on the map, as red diamonds the legend keys (see Map). ## Motion

The map moves only when asked, and every move is one arcing flight (MapLibre's flight path: it pulls out, travels and settles) timed by how far it zooms: 900ms for a short hop, about 2s from the whole country to one city, never more than 2.5s, and a flight back out runs at 0.8 of the flight in. Region and market clicks, making or clearing a pick and a click on the Honolulu inset all fly, and "Show every site" always flies out to the opening view; a new click starts a new flight from wherever the camera is. The zoom buttons step the zoom in 300ms. At zoom 9 the market circles and the sites swap in the same frame. Map labels and marks swap at once when the data changes, with no fade, a hover's preview included. A hover's preview never moves the camera. The stat strip counts up once over 900ms on load. The header's wordmark cursor blinks until the first scroll, then holds still; the slim bar's blinks three times each time the bar slides in, then holds still, so it never pulses beside the text being read (Cody, 2026-10-15); without JS the header's holds still. A pen line draws on left to right in 450ms ease-out (the hero's after a 200ms beat). Four small motions outside the map (Cody, 2026-10-15), each running once or on hover and focus, never hiding text from the markup: (1) a section head's red dash draws left to right (scaleX from 0, from the left) over 180ms the first time it scrolls into view (an IntersectionObserver at 0.6, 10% up from the window's foot, then let go; `section-dash.tsx`); a head already in view when the script runs, a page without JS and print keep it drawn. (2) An open record's SPEC, BRIEF and DETAILS fade up 4px in turn, 200ms each, 40ms apart, the first 40ms after the panel starts to grow (all settled by about 280ms); closing is instant, the bands holding while the panel shuts and resetting unseen. The DETAILS chips no longer stagger on their own, since a chip moving inside a band that moves read as two motions. (3) A company's or school's ↗ nudges 2px up and 2px right in 120ms on hover and keyboard focus, the glyph only, so its underline and the line stay put.

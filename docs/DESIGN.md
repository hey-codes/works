# DESIGN.md

Visual system for codymitch.works (Atlas v26). Source of truth for values: `src/app/globals.css`. This copy is corrected against the v26 design reference; where the two ever disagree, the code wins.

| red | #e10b00 | #ff453a | **structure, an open role's rail, the pen line under the hero name and an open role's name, the AWARD chips, and the map's opening marks** |

The rule: if you can click it, it is blue; if it marks where something begins or belongs, it is red. Never crossed, with two exceptions. (Cody, 2026-10-14) Red also marks the sites Cody opened or worked on opening, on the map (a red diamond, ring or dashed ring, on a site or on the rim of a circle that holds one), in the opening flags of the site card, in the card's NEW LOCATION chip and in the caption's tier chips. Those marks are clickable and still red; nothing else on the map is red. (Cody, 2026-10-15) Red also marks an open role's rail: an open record is structure, its start marked as the section dash and the stat rule are. Red also marks the AWARD chips in DETAILS, outline and text, as an opening flag is; their key stays muted (Cody, 2026-10-15). Red also draws the pen line under the hero name, where the page begins, and under an open role's name, joining its red rail; the pen line a hovered or focused name gets is blue (Cody, 2026-10-15). Focus rings are always blue.

The dark palette is warm charcoal, picked for the dark ground rather than inverted from light. The theme follows the system setting until the user picks one.

The map is the one place Tailwind and shadcn naming appear (`src/app/tailwind.css` maps shadcn's color names onto these tokens). CARTO's country, state and continent names wait for zoom 9, where the market circles give way to sites, so none reads as a circle's name (`regionLabelPlan`). The basemap is CARTO's, recolored from these tokens on every style load, both themes (`groundPaint` in `src/lib/basemap.ts`): land is `land`, the sea `soft` with a 1px coastline in `edge`, parks and land cover flat in the land, built-up areas a trace of ink, borders grey (`edge`, `under`, `line`; CARTO's pink dashes read like the pre-opening ring), roads outlined in `under` and `line` and filled with the land, and CARTO's names `muted` mixed 30% toward the land, a step quieter than the map's own market names. No red or blue in the ground. On the blank ground (CARTO unreachable) there is nothing to recolor.

## Structure patterns

Under 480px the key sits over its content with 4px between them and the content takes the full width, as the role rows' years do, with 12px vertical padding (STACK's CMMS and APPS keys likewise sit over their rows). An open record's SPEC keeps its two columns there, SECTOR | CMMS over FOOTPRINT | BASE, 16px apart, each key over its value and a long value wrapping inside its column (Cody, 2026-10-15).
- **Spacing** (Cody, 2026-10-15, tightened for go-live): 44px above each section head, 32px on phones; 32px between the last section and the footer, 24px on phones. Under 380px on touch the header's boxes draw at 40px, 4px apart, each keeping a 44px hit area (Cody, 2026-10-15).
- **Stat strip:** a 2px red top rule spanning the column, four fixed figures (13, 400+, 2.8M+, 9 Brands) in a 4-column grid sized by container query, mono labels underneath. ## Motion

The map moves only when asked, and every move is one arcing flight (MapLibre's flight path: it pulls out, travels and settles) timed by how far it zooms: 900ms for a short hop, about 2s from the whole country to one city, never more than 2.5s, and a flight back out runs at 0.8 of the flight in. Region and market clicks and opening or closing a role all fly, and "Show every site" flies only when every brand already shows and nothing is open (otherwise it puts the brands back and the camera holds, for exactly one frame change); a new click starts a new flight from wherever the camera is. The zoom buttons step the zoom in 300ms. At zoom 9 the market circles and the sites swap in the same frame. Map labels and marks swap at once when the data changes, with no fade. Hovering a role or tracing a system never moves it. Tint changes take 150ms. The stat strip counts up once over 900ms on load. A pen line draws on left to right in 450ms ease-out (the hero's after a 200ms beat). Four small motions outside the map (Cody, 2026-10-15), each running once or on hover and focus, never hiding text from the markup: (1) a section head's red dash draws left to right (scaleX from 0, from the left) over 180ms the first time it scrolls into view (an IntersectionObserver at 0.6, 10% up from the window's foot, then let go; `section-dash.tsx`); a head already in view when the script runs, a page without JS and print keep it drawn. (2) An open record's SPEC, BRIEF and DETAILS fade up 4px in turn, 200ms each, 40ms apart, the first 40ms after the panel starts to grow (all settled by about 280ms); closing is instant, the bands holding while the panel shuts and resetting unseen. The DETAILS chips no longer stagger on their own, since a chip moving inside a band that moves read as two motions. (3) A company's or school's ↗ nudges 2px up and 2px right in 120ms on hover and keyboard focus, the glyph only, so its underline and the line stay put. (4) On the first load of a visit the hero's base line types in after its dot, a character a step (`steps()` over 600ms on a clip, so its box never changes), starting at 500ms as the hero's pen line lifts off the name: the name is underlined, then the line types, one sequence read top to bottom. The visit is a `sessionStorage` flag set by the head script before paint; a later load, storage that throws, no JS and print show it typed, and screen readers read it whole from the start. Feedback elsewhere takes 150ms or less and state changes 180 to 320ms on `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`. Buttons press. Nothing else fades or moves in on entry. Under reduced motion every camera move is a jump, the dash, bands and base line show their end state at once, the ↗ steps without a glide, and color changes stay.

## Print

Map, motion and controls are removed. Colors forced to light. Everything is expanded into a single column, rows avoid page breaks, with a contact line (site · LinkedIn) in the footer.

## Without JavaScript

The page reads whole: the figures at their values, every role row, the base line typed. Controls that would do nothing are hidden through `data-needs-js` (the head script sets `data-js`): the map frame, its caption staying as when WebGL fails; the theme toggle, so the system theme applies; the PDF note; STACK's hover hint. Role records stay closed (Cody, 2026-10-15).
